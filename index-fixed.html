About JCA

Where Education Meets Personal Attention.

🎓 Welcome to Joshi’s Commerce Academy!

“Today’s Children are Tomorrow’s Future.” This belief has been the source of inspiration behind our journey since 2017.

I am Deepak Joshi, Founder of Joshi’s Commerce Academy. I started this academy with a simple yet powerful vision — to provide quality Commerce education with personal attention, proper guidance and continuous support to every student.

At JCA, we believe that every child is special and every student has the potential to achieve something great. We don’t just teach chapters — we build concepts, develop understanding, encourage confidence and guide students towards their goals.
